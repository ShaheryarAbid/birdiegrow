## TRACK 4 — Ahrefs (buy it; here's exactly what to run)

Worth the spend for one killer use: seeing every backlink your competitors have so you can go get the same ones.

- **Plan:** Ahrefs **Lite (~$129/mo)** is enough to start; upgrade to Standard if you want more historical data.
- **Alternative:** SEMrush is comparable; if choosing one for *backlinks*, Ahrefs has the edge.

**First things to run, in order:**
1. **Link Intersect / Link Gap** — enter `whoosh.io`, `birdigolf.com`, `proagenda.com` as competitors and `birdiegrow.com` as yours. It lists every domain linking to them but **not** you = your target list for Track 3.
2. **Site Explorer** on each competitor → Referring domains → sort by Domain Rating → note the reachable ones (directories, partners, press).
3. **Keywords Explorer** — "golf simulator booking software" + related. Get search volume, Keyword Difficulty, and *which page* of each competitor ranks (so you know what to out-create).
4. **Rank Tracker** — add your 10 target keywords, US + Canada, check weekly. This is how we measure whether any of this is working.
5. **Site Audit** — crawl birdiegrow.com monthly (should be clean given the technical work already done).

**Also free & essential:** in Google Search Console, watch the *Performance → Queries* report for "golf simulator…" terms — that's your real, un-personalized ranking + impressions data.
