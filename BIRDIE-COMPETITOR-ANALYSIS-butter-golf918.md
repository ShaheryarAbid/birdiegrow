# Competitor Analysis — Butter Software & Golf 918 vs Birdie
_July 28, 2026 · data: Ahrefs + live site review_

## TL;DR
Both are **direct competitors** (golf-simulator venue management software) and both are **earlier-stage and weaker than Birdie on every metric that compounds** — domain authority, content depth, organic footprint, and AI-search visibility. Neither is cited by a single AI engine; Birdie is cited 17× by ChatGPT alone. Their headline "ref domain" counts look competitive but are inflated by the **same PBN link spam** hitting Birdie, so ignore them. The one to actually watch is **Golf 918** — it has real hardware/payments/POS integrations and a free trial. Butter is a thin template site and barely a threat today.

## Butter Software (buttersoftware.com)
- **Positioning:** "Indoor Golf Management Software" — bookings, memberships, passes, payments. Targets sim clubs, driving ranges, training centers, multi-location.
- **Pricing:** Starter $199 / Advanced $249 (adds custom mobile app).
- **Integrations:** RemoteLock (door access) is the only real one visible.
- **State of the site:** Single landing-page template, agency-built ("DesignWithMikey"). **Testimonials are placeholder SaaS copy** — "Co-Founder, Gradepie," "intuitive task board," "CMO, Artican" — not real golf customers. No blog, resources, or comparison content → zero organic/AI footprint.
- **Verdict:** Weakest of the three. Not a meaningful threat today. Barely differentiated, and its "cheaper" claim isn't even true ($199 vs Birdie's $197).

## Golf 918 (golf918.com)
- **Positioning:** "Golf Simulator Management Software — the ultimate sim facility solution." Tee sheet, **integrated F&B/restaurant POS (QR mobile ordering)**, membership, loyalty rewards, waitlist/no-show tech.
- **Pricing:** Par $150 / **"Birdie Plan" $250** (yes, they named a tier after you) / Eagle $500 (full restaurant POS), +$25 per additional bay, custom onboarding from $250. **30-day free trial.**
- **Integrations:** **TruGolf (hardware)**, Stripe + Payarc (payments), RemoteLock (door access), QuickBooks (financials).
- **State of the site:** More developed than Butter (About / Software / Help Centre, real FAQ), agency-built ("Studio.909"). Still no blog/resources → zero organic/AI footprint. Testimonial ("Cody Fisher") also looks like placeholder.
- **Verdict:** The more serious competitor. Its edge is **larger facilities with kitchens** (F&B/restaurant POS) and a **TruGolf hardware channel** — segments/angles Birdie doesn't lean into. But it's invisible in search and AI, so it isn't winning demand yet.

## Head-to-head

**Where Birdie wins decisively**
- **Authority:** DR 43 vs 20 / 15.
- **AI search:** 17 ChatGPT citations (+Copilot/AIO/Perplexity) vs **0 for both**. This is a moat — Birdie is the answer the AIs already give.
- **Content:** 80+ indexed pages, real blog/resources/comparison pages, and **real customer video testimonials** vs their placeholder quotes.
- **Trust/optimization:** clean technical SEO, valid schema, GEO answer blocks. They're template sites.

**Where a competitor has an edge**
- **Golf 918 — F&B/restaurant POS + TruGolf integration + QuickBooks:** a real story for larger sim venues with a kitchen. Birdie doesn't emphasize F&B POS.
- **Golf 918 — 30-day free trial:** a lower-friction entry than Birdie's demo-led motion; may surface as a buyer objection.
- **Price optics:** Golf 918's $150 "Par" tier undercuts on the low end (though it caps at 5 bays and adds per-bay fees).

## Recommended moves
1. **Don't chase their ref-domain numbers** — it's shared spam. Keep pressing the content + AI-search moat (roundup outreach, G2/Capterra reviews, Reddit) — it's exactly what neither can match, and it compounds.
2. **Build `/birdie-vs-golf918` (and optionally `/birdie-vs-butter`) comparison pages.** You already have the comparison-page pattern. They have **zero content to defend with**, so you'll own "Golf 918 alternative" and their brand-adjacent queries immediately.
3. **Have a crisp free-trial rebuttal** for Golf 918: white-glove setup, live in 24 hours, month-to-month, no lock-in — lower risk than a trial you configure yourself.
4. **Consider the F&B/POS gap.** If you sell into larger venues with kitchens, a POS/F&B integration story (or at least a comparison talking point) closes Golf 918's main differentiator.
5. **Pursue hardware partnerships (TruGolf, Uneekor, etc.).** Golf 918's TruGolf tie is both a feature and a backlink/distribution channel. Birdie could win the same and more.
