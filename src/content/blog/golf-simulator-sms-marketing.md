Email open rates hover in the teens and twenties. Text messages get opened almost every time, usually within minutes. For a local venue trying to fill a slow Tuesday or bring back a customer who's drifted away, that difference is everything. SMS is the highest-response marketing channel a simulator business has — if you use it well and stay compliant.

## Why texting wins for local venues

Your customers already live in their messages. A booking reminder, a "your bay is ready" note, or a "we miss you, here's 20% off this week" text lands where they'll actually see it. Email is fine for long-form updates, but for time-sensitive, action-oriented nudges — the kind that fill hours *this week* — nothing beats a text. The catch is that texting is also more personal, so it has to be relevant and infrequent, or people opt out fast.

## The flows that actually fill bays

You don't need clever campaigns — you need a few automated flows running quietly in the background. **Booking confirmations and reminders** cut no-shows by making sure people remember they booked. **Post-session review requests** grow your Google review count, which is your best local-search marketing. **Lapsed-customer reactivation** — an automatic text to anyone who hasn't booked in 60+ days — recovers revenue you'd otherwise lose. And **off-peak offers** aimed specifically at your dead weekday hours fill capacity without discounting your busy weekends. ## Consent and compliance, briefly

Texting customers is regulated, and getting it wrong can be costly. The core rule: get clear opt-in consent before you text marketing messages, make opt-out easy (a "reply STOP" that actually works), and don't blast people at odd hours. The cleanest way to handle consent is to capture it at booking or on your waiver, tied to the customer record. This isn't legal advice — check the rules that apply to you — but built-in consent handling is one reason to run SMS through purpose-built software rather than a personal phone.

## Don't do it from your cell

Plenty of operators start by texting customers from their own phone. It doesn't scale, it's easy to forget, there's no consent trail, and it blurs your personal and business life. Automated, compliant SMS tied to your bookings and customer data is the difference between "I keep meaning to text people" and a channel that reliably brings customers back.
