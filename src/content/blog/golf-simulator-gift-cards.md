Gift cards are one of the most underused revenue tools in the simulator business. Sold right, they're prepaid cash in your pocket, a stream of brand-new customers who arrive already paid for, and a reliable spike in December cash flow. Sold wrong — or not at all — you're leaving easy money on the table.

## Why gift cards are better than they look

When someone buys a gift card, you get the cash today for a session that happens later, sometimes much later, and sometimes never. Breakage — the portion of gift cards never redeemed — is pure margin. More importantly, the person receiving the card is almost always a new customer: someone who might never have found you on their own, now arriving with a reason to book. That's customer acquisition someone else paid for.

## Make them easy to buy

The number one reason venues sell few gift cards is that buying one is a hassle. If a customer has to call, visit, or email to get a gift card, most won't bother. Gift cards should be purchasable online in under a minute, delivered instantly by email, and redeemable in your normal [booking flow](/golf-simulator-booking-software/) without a staff member manually applying a code. Friction kills gift-card sales more than anything else.

## Lean into the holidays

December is gift-card season, and simulator time is an easy, appealing present — especially for the golfer who's hard to shop for. Promote gift cards heavily from mid-November through the end of the year, both to your existing customers by text and email and to walk-in traffic. A significant share of a venue's annual gift-card revenue can land in a single six-week window if you actually promote it.

Corporate gifting is the overlooked half of this. Companies buy gift cards in bulk for client thank-yous and employee rewards. A simple "bulk gift cards for your team" offer aimed at local businesses can land large orders you'd never get from individual buyers.

## Manage them without headaches

The operational catch with gift cards is tracking balances and redemptions. Handled manually, it's error-prone and easy to abuse. A [management platform](/golf-simulator-management-software/) that issues, tracks, and redeems gift cards inside the same system as your bookings and payments keeps balances accurate and redemption clean. When the gift card just works at checkout like any other payment, both you and the customer have a better experience.

## Turn recipients into regulars

The real payoff isn't the gift-card sale — it's what happens after redemption. When a gift-card recipient comes in for the first time, that's your moment to convert them into a repeat customer or a member. Capture their contact info at booking, follow up after the session, and invite them back with a specific offer. A gift card that produces one visit is fine; a gift card that produces a member is a win.
