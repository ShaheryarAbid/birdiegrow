Most simulator marketing advice is generic "post on social media" filler. What actually fills bays is a short list of high-leverage tactics built around how people find and choose a local venue. Here are the ones worth your time, roughly in order of return.

## Own local search

When someone searches "golf simulator near me," you want to be the first result and the one with the most reviews. That means a complete, active Google Business Profile with correct hours, photos, and a steady stream of recent reviews. Reviews are the single strongest signal for local ranking and the first thing a prospective customer reads. Automating a review request by text after each session is the highest-ROI marketing you can run — it costs nothing and compounds. Our guide to [Google reviews](/blog/golf-simulator-google-reviews/) covers the full playbook.

## Turn first visits into memberships

A one-time visitor is worth an hour of revenue. A member is worth hundreds of dollars a year and books during hours you'd otherwise leave empty. The most effective on-site marketing you can do is convert happy first-timers into members before they leave. A well-structured [membership program](/blog/golf-simulator-membership-software/) with a clear entry tier does more for annual revenue than any ad campaign.

## Fill weekdays deliberately

Weekends fill themselves. The money is in the empty weekday hours, and those require targeted offers: off-peak pricing, corporate outings, and leagues. Rather than discounting across the board, aim promotions squarely at the slots that are underperforming. Our breakdown of [filling weekday bays](/resources/how-to-fill-weekday-bays-golf-simulator/) goes deeper.

## Run referrals on autopilot

Your happiest customers are your cheapest acquisition channel. A simple "bring a friend, you both get a perk" offer, promoted by text to your existing base, brings in pre-qualified new players. The key is making it automatic and easy to redeem rather than a one-off promotion you forget to run.

## Reactivate lapsed customers

Every venue has a list of people who came a few times and drifted away. Winning one of them back costs a fraction of finding someone new. Segment customers who haven't booked in 60+ days and send a single, specific reactivation offer. A [platform](/golf-simulator-management-software/) that identifies lapsed customers automatically and lets you text them in one click turns a forgotten list into recovered revenue.

## Make booking frictionless

The best marketing in the world fails if booking is a hassle. Every extra step between "I want to play" and a confirmed reservation loses customers. Real-time availability, mobile-friendly booking, and instant confirmation matter more than most operators realize. If your [booking software](/golf-simulator-booking-software/) sends people to a clunky third-party checkout, you're leaking demand you already paid to create.

## What to skip

Broad, untargeted social media posting rarely pays off for a local venue. Neither does discounting your busy hours. Focus your energy on local search, reviews, memberships, and reactivating the customers you already have — that's where the return is.
