Your grand opening is the one moment you'll have the most attention you've ever had for the least effort. Done well, it fills your calendar for weeks, builds a customer list you can market to all year, and plants the reviews that drive your local search. Done as an afterthought, it's a quiet week you never get back. Here's how to make it count.

## Start before you open

The best opening weeks are won in the weeks before. Start building a list the moment you have a location and a date: a simple "opening soon — get early access" page that captures emails and phone numbers, local social posts showing the buildout, and outreach to nearby offices, golf leagues, and community groups. By opening day you want a warm list of people already waiting to book, not an empty room hoping walk-ins show up.

## Make booking the goal, not just attendance

Foot traffic feels good but bookings pay the bills. Set up your [online booking](/golf-simulator-booking-software/) before you announce anything, so every bit of opening buzz drives to a page where people can actually reserve bay time. An opening-week offer — discounted first sessions, a "bring a foursome" deal, or a founding-member membership — gives people a reason to book *now* instead of "sometime." Capture their contact info at booking so opening week becomes a marketing list, not a one-time crowd.

## Run an event, not just an open door

A grand opening event gives the week a focal point and a reason for local press and social sharing. A ribbon-cutting, a longest-drive contest, a free-play happy hour, or a partnership with a local golf pro all create a moment worth talking about. Photos and short videos from the event become content you use for months. If you're planning [corporate events and parties](/blog/golf-simulator-corporate-events/) as a revenue stream, your opening is the perfect time to showcase what a booked event looks like.

## Turn day-one visitors into regulars

The real prize isn't opening-day revenue — it's converting first-timers into repeat customers and members before they leave. Have a clear next step for every visitor: an automated follow-up text thanking them and inviting them back, a founding-member offer, and a review request after their session. A [platform](/golf-simulator-management-software/) that captures contacts and automates that follow-up turns a busy opening into a customer base that keeps booking.

## Get the reviews early

Reviews compound, and the sooner you start, the sooner you rank in local search. Ask every happy opening-week customer for a Google review — ideally with an automated post-session text — and you'll build the review count that makes you the obvious choice for "golf simulator near me."
