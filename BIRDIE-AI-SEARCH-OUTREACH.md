## Notes & sequencing

- **Start with #1 (screengolfers) and #2 (Kisi)** — screengolfers is the one literally feeding the Copilot answer; Kisi is the easiest yes and highest DR.
- **Find the right recipient:** look for an author byline on the article → their email/LinkedIn; otherwise the site's contact form or `hello@`/`editor@` address. Don't guess personal emails.
- **Offer a demo account** to the "tested & reviewed" sites (#6) so the review is real.
- **One polite follow-up** after ~5–7 days if no reply; then move on.
- **Track it:** log site, contact, date sent, reply, and whether Birdie was added. Re-check the Copilot/ChatGPT answer ~2–4 weeks after each add — inclusion in the answer follows inclusion in the sources.
- These are also backlinks — screengolfers (DR28), Kisi (DR72), vteegolf (DR43) all help DR alongside the ranking benefit.
