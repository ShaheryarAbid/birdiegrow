# Competitor analysis — Optix (optixapp.com)

**Date:** 2026-08-15 · Data: Ahrefs (US, settled 2026-08-14; AI citations settled 08-08), live page fetches.

---

## Verdict

Optix is **not** beating Birdie on booking-software search today — it ranks for exactly **one**
golf keyword and gets about **3 organic visits/month** from golf. Judged on the head-to-head
product terms, it barely registers.

But it is the most instructive competitor on the board for two reasons, and the second one
matters more than the first:

1. It is a **DR69 horizontal that has already added golf simulators as a named vertical** —
   solutions page, 4,200-word pillar post, golf customer story. That is a much shorter path to
   the category than the one Birdie is walking, and it can be executed at any time.
2. **Optix is cited on all seven AI engines — including the three Birdie scores 0 on.**
   Gemini 7, Grok 16, Google AI Mode 31. This is the first hard proof in this workstream that
   those engines are reachable, and it shows what the bar looks like.

---

## What Optix is

Coworking / flex-space management software (Vancouver-based, "1,000+ spaces"). Core product:
bookings, invoicing, CRM, white-labeled member mobile app, and a heavily-marketed automation
engine ("1,000+ automations").

It sells by **space type**, not by industry: Coworking · Co-warehousing · Flex · Medical
coworking · **Golf Simulators** · Shared Kitchens · Salons · Recording studios. Golf is one of
roughly eight verticals hanging off one platform.

**Pricing (public, unlike BookedBays):**

| Plan | Monthly | Annual | Users | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Essentials | $229 | **$197/mo** | 50 | white-label app is a paid add-on |
| Pro | $349 | $299/mo | 100 | API, webhooks, visitor mgmt |
| Grow | $579 | $498/mo | 250 | priority support |
| Scale | custom | custom | unlimited | franchises/networks |

Extra locations $68–111/mo; extra users $1.29–1.70/mo.

> **Note the collision:** Optix's entry tier lands on **$197/mo** — the exact number Birdie
> anchors on. Theirs is the annual-billing price of a $229 plan, on a 50-user cap, with the
> white-label app costing extra. Birdie's $197 is the real monthly entry price. That's a
> defensible comparison, but it does mean a buyer who has seen both pages will not experience
> $197 as differentiating.

| **Golf keywords** | **1** | — |
| **Golf organic traffic** | **~3/mo** | — |

\* Ahrefs returns 0 org_traffic/org_keywords for Birdie — the known batch-analysis artifact.
GSC is the real read for Birdie's ranks.

Optix's traffic is overwhelmingly **brand and coworking**: the homepage alone pulls 1,096 of the
1,814 (the term "optix", 2,500/mo, position 1) — roughly 60% brand. The rest is desk booking,
coworking events, coworking design, cowarehousing. Their `/solutions/golf-simulators/` page
ranks for **nothing**.

So on the terms Birdie actually fights for, Optix is absent. It is a coworking company that has
a golf page, not a golf company.

### The real threat: they can turn this on whenever they want
DR69 and 1,672 referring domains mean Optix does not have to earn authority — it already has it,
and can point it at golf content the day it decides golf is worth the effort. Birdie's structural
advantage is *focus*: purpose-built for hourly bays, hour-bank memberships, and unstaffed 24/7
operation with smart-lock door access. Optix's golf page has no equivalent to the door-access
integration lane (Kisi/PDK/RemoteLock) — that stays a genuine moat, and it's also where Birdie's
best backlinks come from.

## Finding #1 — Optix proves the zero engines are winnable

AI citations, same settled date (2026-08-08), same method:

| Engine | Optix | Birdie |
|---|---|---|
| ChatGPT | 63 / 36 pages | 26 / 16 |
| Copilot | 26 / 9 | 9 / 7 |
| Perplexity | 34 / 20 | 7 / 7 |
| **Gemini** | **7 / 7** | **0** |
| **Grok** | **16 / 11** | **0** |
| Google AI Overviews | 12 / 16 | 1 / 2 |
| **Google AI Mode** | **31 / 20** | **0** |

This is the most valuable thing in this analysis. The roadmap's working theory — that Gemini and
Google AI Mode are rankings- and authority-gated, and that Grok needs a different lever — is
consistent with what Optix looks like: DR69, 184 ranking keywords, 55 in the top 3, and a real
content engine (long-form blog, podcast, ebooks, webinars, customer stories).

Optix is not cited because it is clever about schema. It is cited because it **ranks**, across a
wide surface, with depth behind each page. That is the bar. It also means the target is not
"do something Gemini-specific" — it's rankings and breadth, which is what Part 2 already tracks.

## Finding #2 — the content-depth gap is the mechanism, and it's measurable

Head-to-head on the one term where both compete:

| | Optix | Birdie |
|---|---|---|
| Page | `/blog/golf-simulator-business/` | `/resources/golf-simulator-business-guide/` |
| Title | "How to Start a Golf Simulator Business in 2025" | "Golf Simulator Business Guide (2026)" |
| **Word count** | **4,236** | **705** |
| Exact phrase used | **50×** | 5× |
| Position on "golf simulator business" | **17** | not ranking |

Across all 88 Birdie pages the **median is 517 words** and the longest page on the site is
1,648 (`/golf-simulator-booking-software/`). Optix beats Birdie's entire longest page by 2.5×
with one blog post — and that post is what ranks.

Birdie's page inventory is broad and technically flawless (this morning's audit: 0 issues on 88
pages) but **thin**. Breadth has been the strategy; depth is the missing half. This is very
likely a bigger constraint on rankings — and therefore on Gemini/AI-Mode citations — than any
remaining schema or internal-link work.

"Golf simulator business" is **500/mo at keyword difficulty 1**, with 700/mo traffic potential

competing URL, and a measurable reason Birdie is losing it.

## Recommended moves

1. **Rewrite `/resources/golf-simulator-business-guide/` as a genuine pillar** — target
   "golf simulator business" (500/mo, KD 1). Optix wins with 4,236 words; 705 will not do it.
   Pull in the real numbers already scattered across the site (utilization benchmarks, startup
   costs, revenue math, membership structures) and interlink the existing thin resource pages
   into it as a hub. Highest-value single piece of work identified in weeks.
2. **Treat depth as a standing metric.** Median 517 words across 88 pages is the constraint.
   Consider adding a word-count column to the daily audit so thin high-intent pages surface
   automatically rather than being noticed by accident.
3. **Add Optix to the tracked competitor set** in Part 2 `batch-analysis`, but judge it on
   *golf keywords*, not domain totals — same rule as BookedBays. The trigger to care: the day
   `/solutions/golf-simulators/` starts ranking, or golf keywords go above ~5.
4. **Do not chase the $197 collision.** Their $197 is annual-billing on a $229 plan, 50-user cap,
   white-label app extra. If a comparison page is ever warranted, that's the honest framing —
   but Optix is not yet a competitor buyers are shortlisting against Birdie, and a
   `/birdie-vs-optix` page today would be answering a question nobody is asking.
5. **Keep the door-access lane.** It's the clearest product gap in Optix's golf offering and it's
   already Birdie's proven backlink vertical (Kisi DR72, PDK DR60, RemoteLock DR67 pending).
