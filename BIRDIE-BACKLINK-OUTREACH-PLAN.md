# Birdie Backlink Outreach Plan

**Goal:** earn 3–6 quality, relevant backlinks per month to push "golf simulator booking software" (KD 1) from page 3 to page 1, and lift Domain Rating from 43.

**The one principle:** relevance + authority beats volume. Five links from golf/SaaS sites Google trusts will do more than 500 spam ".store" domains (the exact junk that hit you last week — ignore it, never buy links). Every link should come from a page a real golf operator might actually read.

**How progress is measured:** your morning report already flags new/lost referring domains and DR each day via Ahrefs. Watch **referring domains gained/month** and the rank of the money term. Realistic: meaningful movement in 60–90 days.

---

## The system (do this, or the list below dies)

Backlinks fail when outreach is sporadic. Make it a standing habit:

- **5 outreach touches per week** (~15 min/day, or one 75-min block). That's ~20/month → at a 15–25% reply/win rate, ~3–5 links/month.
- **Track every touch** in a simple sheet with columns: `Target | URL | Type | Contact | Date sent | Follow-up date | Status | Link live?`
- **One follow-up** per target, 5–7 days after the first email. Two total touches, then move on.
- **Batch by type** (do all the hardware partners one week, all the podcasts the next) so you reuse the same template.

I can add a weekly "backlink outreach" nudge to your morning report, and it already tells you when a new link lands.

## Tier 2 — Golf industry media & orgs (light pitch)

Relevance gold. A single link from golf.com or NGF is worth dozens of generic links.

| Target | Angle |
|---|---|
| **National Golf Foundation (ngf.org, DR 72)** | Industry body; pitch a data contribution or membership/vendor listing |
| **Golf.com (80), Golf Inc Magazine, Golf Business News** | Pitch a guest article or data piece: *"Weekday utilization benchmarks for indoor simulator venues"* |
| **Sports Business Journal (82)** | Pitch the "indoor golf is booming" business angle with your operator data |
| **GolfGenius, ClubhouseOnline** | Golf-software peers — explore a partner/content cross-link |
| **Indoor-golf / golf-entrepreneur podcasts** | Pitch yourself as a guest (founder story + "how to fill weekday bays"); show notes = a link |

**Your unfair advantage here:** you sit on real operator data (utilization, weekday fill rates, revenue-per-bay). A short original **"State of Indoor Golf Simulator Venues" data post** on your own blog becomes link-bait that these outlets cite — turning one piece of content into recurring links. Highest-leverage content you can make.

## Tier 3 — Ongoing / earned (compounding, low effort)

- **Qwoted (qwoted.com), Featured (featured.com), Source of Sources** — the HARO successors. Answer 2–3 reporter queries/week as a "golf tech / small-business founder" source. Each accepted quote = a press link.
- **Reddit r/Golfsimulator, r/golf; Facebook "Indoor Golf Business Owners" groups** — mostly no-follow, but they drive referral traffic, brand mentions, and the AI-citation signal. Be genuinely helpful, never spammy.
- **Customer & partner mentions** — every venue that uses Birdie is a potential "powered by Birdie" link on their own site. Ask happy customers.

## Do NOT do
- Don't buy links or use PBNs/fiverr "SEO packages" (that ".store" blast you saw is exactly this — worthless at best, toxic at worst).
- Don't mass-email irrelevant sites. One relevant golf link > 100 generic ones.
- Don't disavow the spam yet — Google ignores most of it; only upload the disavow file if rankings actually drop.
